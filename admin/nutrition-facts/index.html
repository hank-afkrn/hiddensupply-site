Barcode Module
Generate or validate a barcode for your label. Enter a UPC-A or EAN-13 code you own - or use placeholder mode for mockups. Placeholder barcodes are clearly marked and blocked from Manufacturer Ready exports.
ⓘ Barcode Ownership Notice

This tool renders barcode images from codes you provide. It does not mint GS1-registered barcodes or grant retail ownership. To sell in U.S. retail, you need a GS1 Company Prefix and properly assigned UPC codes. Placeholder/Demo mode is for mockups only - clearly marked and export-blocked.
